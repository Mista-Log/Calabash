# Calabash (Knowledge)

**Calabash (Knowledge)** is a university-focused digital library designed to help students and lecturers store, organize, and access academic materials seamlessly. Inspired by the cultural significance of the *calabash* as a vessel for preservation and sharing, this platform serves as a modern container for academic knowledge.

---

## 🧠 Overview

University learning materials are often scattered across messaging apps, emails, and personal storage, making them hard to find and manage.  
Calabash (Knowledge) solves this problem by providing a **centralized, structured, and visually engaging digital library** for academic resources.

The platform introduces an innovative **3D visualization system** that allows students to explore all semester materials in an intuitive and interactive way.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Centralize access to lecture materials and past questions
- Improve academic continuity across student generations
- Provide a clean, intuitive dashboard for students and lecturers
- Enhance engagement through optional 3D material visualization
- Support structured, semester-based learning

---

## 👥 Target Users

- Undergraduate students
- Postgraduate students
- University lecturers
- Academic departments (future expansion)

---

## ✨ Key Features

### Students
- Access lecture notes and PDFs by course and semester
- View and download past questions from previous sessions
- Search and filter academic materials
- Explore materials using a 3D visualization view
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop

### Lecturers
- Upload and manage lecture materials
- Organize content by course and semester
- Update or replace outdated materials easily

### System
- Role-based authentication (Student / Lecturer / Admin)
- Secure file storage and access control
- Clean and scalable architecture

---

## 🧩 MVP Scope (Hackathon)
